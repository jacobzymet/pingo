# Pingo desktop

Mode: Operate. Follow the OS light/dark preference automatically.

Direction: a focused native sharing workspace. One transfer surface, quiet device
context, and progressive disclosure for adapter inventory, VPN options and diagnostics.
Use a neutral canvas, opaque elevated surfaces, restrained blue controls, generous
spacing, and readable native typography. No decorative metrics or permanent sidebars.

Message/File/Receive is the primary interaction. The selected peer and a representative
local IPv4 remain visible. Expand the local address disclosure for every adapter and
assigned IPv4. Activity remains inline; packet details and setup checks are collapsed.
Preserve keyboard shortcuts, explicit stop/recovery, safety checks, and all functionality.
